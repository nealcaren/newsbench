Winnington, Worcester Regiment, commanding the Northamptonshire Regiment, who has fallen in action, was born in 1876, and entered the Worcestershire Regiment from the Militia in 1897, becoming captain in 1901, major in 1914, and Lient.-colonel (brevet) in June, 1916. He saw service in the South African War in 1899-1900, and was at the relief of Ladysmith, including the operations at Spion Kop, Vaal Kranz, the Tugela Heights, and Pieter's Hill, as well as one operations in the Orange Free State and those in Cape Colony south of Orange River. During the present campaign he has been repeatedly mentioned in despatches, and was the recipient of the D.S.O. in recognition of his services. Lient.-Colonel Winnington married Joyce Mary, daughter of Mr. D. Marriage, of Bouchton Park, Worcester. A keen aff. round sportsman, he excelled in cricket, and had played for his county.
