Mansion House - 1st Woman Suffrage Party meeting, Ward I - Dist. I.
FIFTH SECTION.
THE PROVIDENCE SUNDAY JOURNAL, FEBRUARY 16, 1913.

W

At the opening of the meeting, which
was well attended, Miss Helen Thomas
was elected captain in the district, to
have direction of the details of the work
for the cause. A discussion of the meth-
ods used by suffrage workers in New
York, Chicago and other cities followed,
after which the members mapped out
plans for a similar campaign here.
Fri. Jan 10, 1912
