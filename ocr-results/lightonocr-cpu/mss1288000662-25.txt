the purpose of electing officers. All  
officers elected and appointed at the  
first organization gathering in 1916,

hold original offices and are active  
promoters. A vote of confidence is  
taken for these representatives every  
year, and it has proved that all votes  
are unanimously favorable. The law-  
making bodies of the T. D. L. Asso-
