Jane Addams the best friend of all of us, the one who believed that we had a right to dream, has gone, yet she is not gone because her spirit and the impression of the life she lived is with us and will be always. I went to Washington and attended everything in her honor, for I felt that it might be the last opportunity. The first triumph was the dinner where over one thousand people ate together in her honor. She was present and was her own gay, lovely, charming self.

The day following the dinner for Miss Addams was to be a wonderful out-of-door peace meeting, when for the first time a peace message was broadcast over the ocean. The greetings came from England, France, Russia and Japan. The weather was uncertain, it rained a bit and Miss Addams was prevented from appearing. But it was arranged for her to receive the message.
