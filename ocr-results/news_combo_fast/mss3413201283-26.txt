Mrs. Algeo Outlines Plans
Furthering Movement in
this City.

A plan to swing Pawtucket into the
onward march of equal suffrage was
outlined by Mrs. James W. Algeo of
Providence to a sathering of Paw-
tucket women in the parlor of Miss
Althea L. Halls home at 188 High
street, last night. If this plan is fol-
lowed out the equal suffrage adherents
in this city will have six ward leaders,
three captains in each ward, monthly
meetings in various wards, meetings of
leaders to shape the general plan of
campaign, and public meetings, (indoors
or out) as the occasion may require,
with prominent speakers to help boost
the cause.

The leaders and captains will be fur-
nished with guide books siving rules
for successful organization. Step by
step, these books will point the way
to a machine as strong and powerful
by comparison as the Tammany organ-
ization of New York or the anti-Demo-
cratic Society of this city.

Woe to the candidate for office who
openly opposes equal suffrage in these
parts. Working along strictly political
lines and using well-defined political
methods invented by men, the local
suffragists will set him up as a target
and bring all possible pressure to bear
against his candidate.
