Lient-Colonel J. F. S. Winninton, Worcester Regiment, commanding the Northamptonshire Regiment, who has fallen in action, was born in 1876, and entered the Worcestershire Regiment from the Militia in 1897, becoming captain in 1901, major in 1914, and lieut.-colonel (brevet) in June, 1916. He saw service in the South African War in 1899-1900, and was at the relief of Ladysmith, including the operations at Spion Kop, Vaal Kranz, the Tugela Heights, and Pieter's Hill, as well as the operations in the Orange Free State and those in Cape Colony south of Orange River. During the present campaign he has been repeatedly mentioned in despatches, and was the recipient of the D.S.O. in recognition of his services. Lieut.-Colonel Winninton married Joyce Mary, daughter of Mr. D. Marriott, of Broughton Park, Worcester. A keen all-round sportsman, he excelled in cricket, and had played for his county.

Major Aubrey Hugh Darnell, D.S.O., Australian Imperial Forces, who has been wounded, is the second son of the late the Rev. F. A. Darnell, senior chaplain to the Forces in Ireland, and of Mrs. Darnell, of Bellevue Park, Cork. He was born in May, 1886, and had his commission in the first contingent of the Australian Expeditionary Forces. During the war he had received the Companionship of the D.S.O.
