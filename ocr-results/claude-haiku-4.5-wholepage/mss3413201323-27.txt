**FRANKLIN ROOSEVELT'S EXCHANGE.**

Col. Watterson, editor of the Louisville Courier-Journal, says that he received a letter from Franklin D. Roosevelt, assistant secretary of the navy, expressing concern about Watterson's recent editorial utterances about the administration.

Watterson replied with a letter containing some rather hot language about certain naval affairs, and when the Roosevelt letter reached him a second time, Watterson wrote back: "I don't know what you want me to do. Don't you know I'm for the Constitution of the United States?"
