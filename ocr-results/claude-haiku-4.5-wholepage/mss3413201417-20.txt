BE TRUE.

Man of the North-land! be true to the Right!
Pass onward unflinching, and shrink from the fight!
The cause of the slave must not fail in thy hand,
But gird up thy loins for the battle so grand.

Oh, say, shall we trample our brothers cast down,
Shall avarice goad us to battle and frown?
Shall we, who have purchased our freedom so dear,
By our contract men unto this troubled sphere?

And why are we silent while millions are sold?
Why not an end utterly crying out bold?
With hearts firm and fixed, unlinching and true,
We'll demand the redemption of all of the crew.

We ask not for one till our labor is done;
We must stand for the right till the light and the dark,
And our hearts will not fail till the cause is complete,
And their memory still in our hearts we will meet.

Oh, are pledged to the true in the brows where they fled!
To bear a noble mind to our whole world with zeal!
In truth and in justice to nobly stand fast—
And their memory still in our hearts we will hold.
