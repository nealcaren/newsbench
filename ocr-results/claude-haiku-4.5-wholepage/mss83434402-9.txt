Major A. H. Ashton, D.S.O., received the following despatch:—Major A. H. Ashton, D.S.O. (who was in command of the Northamptonshire Regiment, who fell into action near Lens), has been reported as dead, having died of wounds received in action. He was a major in 1914, and died colonel (brevet) in June 1916. He saw service in the South African War in 1900-1901, at the relief of Ladysmith, the advance to Pretoria, the relief of Kimberley, Vaal Kreis, the Tugela Heights, and Pieter's Hill, as well as the operations in the Orange Free State and Cape Colony. He served in the European War with the Northamptonshire Regiment, and is believed to have been killed at the actions near Lens referred to above. Major Ashton, High Daisai, D.S.O., was a fellow of the Imperial Military College, Camberley, and resided at York. He entered the Army in 1885, and had his commission in the first contingent of the South African appointment. After the war he had received the Companionship of the D.S.O.
