TWO MEN KILLED BY ROCKVILLE CAR

Smashes Into Buggy—Fleischer and Richard Samich Dead.

(Special to The Courant.)
Rockville, March 3.

Paul Fleischer, 41, and Richard Samich, 35, were struck by a trolley car and killed this morning on the Rockville-New Hartford car line. The accident happened about 11:15 a.m. at a point on the road between Pineville and the town of Rockville on the Pineville section, in a buggy driving to Ellington, and apparently did not see the car was reached a point about a quarter mile in front of the car. The animal escaped but the men were struck a fatal blow.

Members of the Lane family rushed to the assistance and Dr. T. M. Loughlin of Rockville was called. Dr. S. Hanna of Pineville also rendered. The latter being the medical examiner. They examined the bodies and pronounced the injuries instantaneous. Mr. Lamsch had a hole in his head and his ribs were broken. Mr. Fleischer was badly bruised about the face.

Those who witnessed the accident said the trolley car was in the path of the car, and although the motorman rang his bell and whistled, the driver was unable to bring the team to a stop. The car was in charge of Conductor Lane Dewey, and the motorman was under the direction of Motorman John Brennan who has been in the employ of the company for about a year and a half, and, taking all around, has done good work.
