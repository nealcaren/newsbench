RATIONALITY.

Ma. Editor.—Dear Sir.—Rationality, under the title of anti-supernaturalism, is a system addressed in an address delivered before the District School on Wednesday, by Rev. W. D. Wilson. This system claims to be the only cause for September, 1861. I beg leave, in behalf of the Rationalists here, to say a few words in explanation of the system, as far as we may be, the cause which it disputes.

My general objection to the definition of Rationality, as stated in the address, is this: it is neither rationality, nor, self-cult, nor for me, nor rationality: nor for the dead.
