Teachers Federation
Gathers to Discuss Future
CHICAGO—Officials of the
American Federation of Teach-
ers announced today that their
federation’s 33rd annual con-
vention will be held in St.
Louis, July 28-31. Delegates
will be elected at the local
level.
Speakers at the convention
will include Allan S. Hay,
state superintendent of public
instruction of Illinois. Topics
considered to be of most
concern, according to Federa-
tion members, will be teacher
standards, teacher salary sched-
ules, and the extension of col-
lective bargaining rights to
teachers in all states.

Bill to Prohibit Jobless
Handbills Ready for Action
ALBANY—Assemblyman Will-
iam R. Dunham of Albany has
introduced a bill in the State
Assembly to prohibit the dis-
tribution of handbills by un-
employed persons, unless a
permit is obtained from the
police department of the city or
village in which the handbills
are to be distributed.

LONG BEACH — Forest
Whitaker, who is studying to
become a professional musician
at Long Beach State at the
Navy Pier campus, was named
California’s most valuable play-
er in college baseball this week.
Whitaker, a senior from Oak-
land, was a starting pitcher for
the 49ers and had an earned
run average of 1.65 in 13 games.

The Senate unanimously passed
a bill this week to provide for the
establishment of a national aero-
nautics and space administration
as a part of the Department of
Defense. The bill now goes to the
House of Representatives. The ad-
ministration believes the bill will
accelerate the American space pro-
gram, President Eisenhower has
pointed out.

The Old Heidelberg associa-
tion for the benefit of the
Russian War Relief chapter,
will hold a gala dinner-dance
on Saturday evening at the
Ramblewood Country Club.
Mrs. Viola D. Jacobs is chair-
man of the event. Accord-
ing to reports, the gala will
feature the music of Dick
Hyman and His Orchestra.
Cocktail hour will begin at
6:30 p.m. The menu will be
prepared in the German tra-
dition and will consist of
Roast Pig, Sauerbraten and
Black Forest Cake.
