New York, June 30, 1881.—The Commercial Bulletin
reports: The produce markets generally closed steady with
much the same general features noted in last report,
there being no lack of business in staple producing lines,
with the total transactions in the product to exceed that
until about the middle of next month, but plain and coi-
nating prices throughout the list, the market closing on
account of back orders, which abroast to a great extent
the holiday feeling that is anticipated. Prices are with-
out quotable change and firm at rolling rates.

COAL RATES FOR JULY.
PHILADELPHIA, PENN., June 20.—In our July circular,
giving prices for coal for eastern shipments, was issued
today, fixing rates for this month. Rates for steam
ash coal delivered on board vessels at Port Richmond for
coastwise markets are based on New York and Boston
usual terms—between New York and Boston, $1.50;
$2.55 for chestnut and $2.85 for pez. The prices at Eliz-
abethport, N. J., are 25c higher on each grade.
