IRISH EVICTIONS.

LONDON, June 30, 1881.—A flying column con-
sisting of mounted constables and police, arrested
the sheriff in enforcing evictions at Mitchels-
town, Cork county. Today. The persons against
whom evictions were issued barricaded their houses
with stone protecting their doors. Stones were
placed across several roads in order to delay the
progress of the troops, which, on the reaching the
town, found a number of carts thrown across the
streets. The military and police cleared the
crowd until the streets were deserted. It is
stated that thirty evictions were resisted.
