IRISH EVICTIONS.
LONDON, June 30, 1881.–A flying column con-
ducted by magistrate attended with forces, assisted
the sheriff in enforcing evictions at Mitchells-
town, Cork county, today. The persons against
whom they had been issued resolved to make de-
fences, stone-throwing and throwing boiling water
placed across several roads in order to delay the
advance of the foreign police, up to enter the
town, found a number of carts thrown across the
streets. The eviction was carried on all
crowd until the streets were deserted. It is
stated that thirty persons were arrested.

RIVAL ROADS IN COURT.
SANTA FE, NEW MEXICO, June 30, 1881.–The
war of the Santa Fe and the Southern Pacific
is still going on. Ex-Secretary of the Interior
Schurz and counsel for General A. A. Williams
of Kansas have arrived, and are counsel for the
Santa Fe. The Southern Pacific have removed
their case, making a tender of $8000 in gold for
the land in dispute and claim the right to be en-
titled thereto, which is denied. The defendants
filed into the Superior Court an amended legal
grant; state that the plaintiffs have not complied
with the conditions, and that the plaintiffs there-
by allowed the land to be sold, while the plain-
tiffs in the plaintiffs have made an offer to
pay $8000 in settlement of the case, which was de-
nied by the court, and offered to put up $1,000,-
000 in government bonds and other security as a
guarantee of good faith. Judge Bristol continued
the case until 1 of September, when the plaintiffs
will produce counter-affidavits to answer the de-
fendant's.
