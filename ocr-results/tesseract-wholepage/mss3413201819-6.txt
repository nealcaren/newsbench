Little Brothers
to Frolic in Arb

Oberlin “big brothers” and
their little brothers from the
Children’s Home will go for an
outing at the Arb next Sunday
afternoon, Chairman Bob Gass-
ler revealed yesterday.

The outing will be the first step
in a much-enlarged program for
the little brothers this year, Gass-
ler declared. Plans call for pic-
nics, more outings, ball games,
and parties for the children.

The big brother organization
is a branch of the Young Men’s
Christian Association designed
primarily to furnish outside ac-
tivities to children in the Home.

All upperclassmen and «fresh-
men who have little brothers are
urged to meet in the Men’s Build-
ing before three o’clock on next
Sunday.

TEACHER’S FEDERATION

URGES JAPANESE BOYCOTT
CHICAGO — Officials of the

American Federation of Teachers

announced today that their or-
ganization has endorsed a nation-
wide boycott of Japanese products
and urged all Americans to join

it.

“Let the leaders of Japan know
that we hate war, that we detest
aggressors, and that we know how
to deal with them,” said Dr. Geo.
Artelle, vice-president of the fed-
eration.
