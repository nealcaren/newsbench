Ella M. Sherwin Speaks Before League of Women Voters

Monday, March 12, the League of Women Voters of New York City held its annual convention at Hotel Pennsylvania. Mrs. Ada R. Wolff had been asked to speak on Industrial Equality principles, but was unable to do so because of a previous engagement, and Miss Ella M. Sherwin, corresponding secretary of the Women's Equal Opportunity League, took up the verbal cudgels in defense of our firm stand

Miss Sherwin is a member of the League of Women Voters of Brooklyn and her uncompromising attitude against 8-hour and minimum wage bills for women is concurred in by many other women who are members of the Women Voters League, in this city and elsewhere. Miss Sherwin's speech was listened to with much interest by her audience, and after the meeting many women told her they believed she was right.
