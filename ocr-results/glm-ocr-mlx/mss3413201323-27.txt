Washington Bureau of The Des Moines Register: In an interview Senator John F. Shafroth of Colorado vigorously champions the cause of equal suffrage, and specifically defends woman suffrage in his own state against charges recently made by J. B. Maling of Denver that equal suffrage had proved a failure in Colorado.

"My attention has been directed to statements made by Mr. Maling and published in Iowa papers, to the effect that woman suffrage in Colorado was a failure and should be abandoned," said Senator Shafroth.
