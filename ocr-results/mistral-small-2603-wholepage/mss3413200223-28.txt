## THE LIBERATOR.

### NOVEMBER 18.

### THE NATION'S JUDGMENT.

#### BY W. C. GANNITT, ESQ.

O faithful minds, who, in the face of all
Look for God's judgment, mixing evil and good,
Not on the future, but your present state!
Not on the future, but your present state!
Not on the future, but your present state!
Not as ye dream, on supernatural ground.
Not as ye dream, on supernatural ground.
But borne in battle-smoke upon the land.

This is a truth hard to digest, yet truth
The Devil's engines work to gain as Heaven's
Nor is the Judgment-day—and Hell has uttered
The cry of the lost, and Heaven has heard it,
Owed with in, to purity by fire and flood.
We watch the earth's unburied slain and sulphur's black;
We hear the bells from trench and battlement;
The air is thick with the smoke of battle;
The earth is red with the blood of men.
And angels—we who are to follow Christ
In deeds of love and justice, now must gush
Our teeth in torment, with spirits spurned and seared.
The spiritual law which kills all men
And dries up every human heart and mind.
And yet as soon expect earth's heart
To still the beatest monster's growth,
And never a tempest rise to purge the air,
