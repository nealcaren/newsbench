### Salutatory

The Maine Association Opposed to Suffrage for Women speaks for the majority of the women of Maine. This the suffragists in effect admitted when they failed to provide for a referendum when they failed to provide for a referendum when they failed to provide for a referendum when they failed to provide for a referendum.

Theoretical objections may be offered from both sides. The antis may object that, if every woman in Maine were to declare for woman suffrage, the objections to such a policy arising from consideration of the limitations Nature has imposed upon her functions, and from consideration of her natural and proper relation to the family, society and the state, would remain unaffected and in full force.

So also the suffragists may object that the suffrage is a natural right, wherever it belongs to every man, woman or child whether he or she chooses to use it wisely, unwisely or not at all, and without regard to whether the women in the state who wish to go into politics are many or few.

Neither side could consent to leave the question to the decision of a referendum by the women of the state without surrendering theoretical objections to such a course.

The fact remains, however, that either side is practical enough to favor any method of decision which would help it towards its goal, and that the practical objection by the suffragists to their knowledge that it would be a referendum by the women is that it would be a referendum by the women.
