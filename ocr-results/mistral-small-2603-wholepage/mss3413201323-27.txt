### THE PARAMOUNT ISSUE.

Some Iowa newspapers carry the erroneous impression that the special law enforcement agents are appointed by the attorney general. The special officers are appointed by the governor. If Iowa should elect a liberal governor he probably would appoint liberal special officers. Should a conservative governor be elected the special officers would be conservative.

### MR. IRISH GROWING OLD.

MR. IRISH is said in this work to have changed his views on the suffrage question as they shift from many men as they shift from boyant enthusiasms of youth to the calm and sober views of age.

We do not know of an illustration more to the point than the speeches of John P. Irish. He is now making in Iowa against woman suffrage when contrasted with the speeches he made in the Iowa legislature when he was a young man.

John P. Irish was a forward looking man in the old days when with spontaneous eloquence he pleaded for equal rights. Now he is as solemnly alarmed as any other pessimist.

It is really too bad that so many estimable people begin to see every man's duty in his own light when "ambition leans most to thee."
