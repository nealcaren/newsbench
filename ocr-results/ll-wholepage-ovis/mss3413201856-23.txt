At the regular meeting of the common council, last evening, the report of the committee on salaries, to whom was recommitted the report and orders establishing the salaries, that the salary of the city treasurer be at the rate of $8,000 per annum, with a further sum of 1,000 as treasurer of the sinking funds, and that he be also allowed not exceeding$17,400 to be paid to his regular clerks, came up from the board of aldermen, with an amendment to the order, fixing the salary of the city treasurer at $6500. Mr. Parkman of Ward 9 moved that the matter be laid on the table, so that no action shall be taken until the entire salary bill is presented for consideration. This motion was subsequently withdrawn and then Mr. Whitmore of Ward 12 said that he was not to be the salary bill as a whole, and not to attempt to raise the salaries of a few persons at the expense of the mass of city officials; he therefore moved to substitute for the report the committee of salaries that was adopted last year. After a running discussion, the motion to substitute prevailed by a vote of 31 to 25. The council then took up the several orders establishing the salaries of individual officers, but, after fixing the salary of the mayor, further consideration of the subject was assigned to the next meeting.
