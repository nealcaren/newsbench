TEACHER'S FEDERATION
URGES JAPANESE BOYCOTT

CHICAGO — Officials of the
American Federation of Teachers
announced today that their or-
ganization has endorsed a nation-
wide boycott of Japanese products
and urged all Americans to join
it.

"Let the leaders of Japan know
that we hate war, that we detest
aggressors, and that we know how
to deal with them," said Dr. Geo.
Artelle, vice-president of the fed-
eration.

transcriptions.

the Coming Century."
