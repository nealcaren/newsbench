The wool market, reviewed in detail elsewhere, has been fairly active, the sales footing up 2,673,188 lbs, against 2,881,523 lbs last week, or a decrease of about 200,000 lbs, most of which is in foreign wool. The sales, as collected from the several houses and given in detail below, show a decrease of about 200,000 lbs, most of which is in foreign wool, the decrease of that being 137,000 lbs.
